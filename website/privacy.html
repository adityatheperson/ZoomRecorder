Local-first privacy

Your lectures stay on your computer.

Zoom Recorder is designed to create and organize recordings without accounts, advertising, analytics, or cloud transcription.

What remains local

Your MP4 recordings, class library, editable transcripts, processing checkpoints, and downloaded Whisper model are stored under your Windows user’s local application-data folders. Updating the portable app does not delete that information.

What uses the internet

The application uses the internet to open Zoom meetings and to download and verify the English Whisper model the first time you request transcription. The model is approximately 500 MB. Your recording is not uploaded as part of transcription.

No OpenAI connection

The current release does not require or read an OpenAI API key, does not upload recordings to OpenAI, and does not call OpenAI services. Transcription runs through a packaged local worker.

This website

This static website uses no accounts, cookies, contact forms, advertising scripts, or visitor analytics. GitHub may process standard service logs when serving the site or release files under its own policies.

Recording permission

You are responsible for obtaining permission before recording. Follow your instructor’s rules, institutional policies, and applicable law. Zoom Recorder is a tool; it does not determine whether a recording is permitted.
